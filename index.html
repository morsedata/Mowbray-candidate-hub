Voting Platform FAQ

Answers to frequently asked questions regarding ballot access and security.

How do I access the online voting platform? +

Eligible voters receive a unique Voter ID and Access Key via SMS or registered email. Click the "Cast Ballot Online" button above to enter your credentials and cast your vote.

How many candidates can I vote for? +

You may select up to 11 candidates on your ballot. You are not required to fill all 11 slots to submit a valid ballot.

Is my vote private and anonymous? +

Yes. The platform verifies voter eligibility via your Access Key, but your specific candidate selections are encrypted and detached from personal identifiers.

What if I did not receive my Voter ID or Access Key? +

If you are a registered plot holder or Mowbray resident in good standing, please contact the electoral helpline or cemetery office to verify your details on the voter roll.
